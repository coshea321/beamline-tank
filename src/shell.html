Sectors

Field manual
Controls
Arrows or WASD drive (first press turns, second moves). Space fires. U undoes, R restarts. Tap a square to drive there by the safest route; tap your tank to fire.
Goal
Drive onto the yellow flag.
Brick
Destroyed by one shot.
Crate
Each shot pushes it one square. In water it becomes a bridge.
Mirror
Bends the beam off its bright diagonal. A hit on a dull side pushes it. It sinks in water.
Round mirror
Fixed in place. A hit on a dull side turns it clockwise.
Glass
A framed window. Blocks tanks, lets lasers through.
Anti-tank
Shoots you on sight along its barrel line. Killed only by a hit on the muzzle; other hits push it.
Water
Sinks your tank.
Ice
Pale floor with streaks, no frame. Tanks and pushed objects slide until stopped. Cracked ice turns to water after you leave it.
Belt
Carries the tank in the arrow direction.
Tunnel
Teleports to the matching tunnel if its exit is clear.
